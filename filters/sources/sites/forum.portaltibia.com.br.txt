! ===== Forced redirect (interaction hijack) =====
! Observed 2026-10-21 (Chrome Beta, ads allowed, no clicks): after ~23s the tab was navigated to
! www.tibiaring.com/char.php?... then tibiaring.com. Nothing in the site's own HTML/JS references
! tibiaring; the top navigation had no script stack (initiator "other"), consistent with an ad
! creative in a same-origin (friendly) GPT iframe setting top.location. Not reproducible in 3x90s
! later runs (rotating creative). Fixed by the ad-stack rules above: with them, no ad frame loads.
! (A tibiaring subframe block was tested and dropped: Chrome already stops cross-origin frame-busting
! without a user gesture, so it would not have prevented this.)
