# Code of Conduct

Tibia Anti-Ad welcomes contributors regardless of experience, identity, background or preferred way to play. Be respectful, specific and constructive in issues, pull requests, reviews and other project spaces.

Discuss evidence, software behavior and monetization practices. Do not harass fansite owners, streamers, contributors or users. Personal attacks, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexual harassment, spam and coordinated abuse are unacceptable. Criticism of advertising never authorizes targeting a person or disrupting a service.

Respect privacy: redact captures and ask before publishing someone else's private communications. Accept corrections and focus disagreements on reproducible facts.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report concerns to repository maintainer [@nesleykent](https://github.com/nesleykent). For sensitive incidents, open an issue requesting a private contact channel without publishing personal details. Do not use security advisories for ordinary conduct disputes.

The maintainer may edit or remove harmful content, close discussions, warn participants or restrict participation. Responses should reflect the severity, repetition and impact of the behavior. Reports will be handled with discretion; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed on a public platform. An affected participant may request reconsideration with relevant context through the same contact route.
